Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Email roguebrokergame@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version for bugs.

How does the free play work?

Calcade gives you 7 minutes of play time for free. The timer only runs while you are playing. When it runs out, you can unlock the full game with a one-time purchase of $4.99. There are no ads and no subscription.

I already bought the game. How do I get it back?

Open the paywall or Settings and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Purchases are also shared to your other devices on that Apple Account.

Will I lose my progress if I close the app?

No. Your run is saved after every move, so you can close the app at any time and pick it up again with Continue.

Is my progress synced between devices?

Not yet. Progress and unlocks are stored on the device you play on.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
