Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Calcade is a game made by Jacob Tichenor ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: Calcade does not collect any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Calcade has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking or SDKs. We do not collect, store or share your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers or usage data.

Information stored on your device

To work, the game saves a few things only on your device: your current run and progress (unlocks and statistics), your settings (sound, haptics, display finish), and how much free play time you have used. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the app removes your progress and settings. The record of used free play time is kept in your device's Keychain so it is not reset by reinstalling.

Purchases

The one-time "Calcade Full Game" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether the purchase was made, so the game can unlock. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Calcade is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Because we collect no data, changes are unlikely to affect you.
